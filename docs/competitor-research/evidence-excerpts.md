## poparide-booking

Requested: https://www.poparide.com/en-ca/help/how-to-book

Resolved: https://support.poparide.com/en-ca/articles/9704459-how-to-book-on-a-posted-trip-as-a-passenger

> Before booking, check all the trip details, including the driver’s profile, the pick-up and drop-off points, and any stops. Review the driver’s rating, preferences (e.g., music, pet-friendly, etc.), and if the trip is Instant book or requires approval from the driver.

> Have questions? You can message the driver in advance to clarify any trip details missing. If the trip doesn’t work for you, we suggest looking for another option that better suits your needs.

> Note: All passengers are expected to meet at the driver’s designated pick-up location.
