## Full description — both stores

Travel between Montréal, Longueuil, Ottawa and Gatineau with Ridely.

Choose “I’m a passenger” to search for a ride, or “I’m a driver” to post and manage your departures. The app keeps Home, Search, Post, Cart and Profile easy to reach.

FOR PASSENGERS
• Review the departure time, standard meeting points, seat fare, vehicle and luggage allowance.
• Request your seats. Your reservation is confirmed when the driver accepts.
• After acceptance, message your driver and request custom pickup or drop-off where offered. Extra fees and driver approval apply.
• Find your trip details and private boarding code in the app.
• Leave a star review after both travellers confirm the completed trip.

FOR DRIVERS
• Prepare a private ride draft before completing setup.
• Complete email confirmation, identity verification, and separate licence-eligibility and vehicle-insurance reviews before publishing.
• Choose meeting points, seat availability, luggage allowance and optional custom-stop fees.
• Accept passenger requests and manage your booked trips.
• Review passengers after confirmed completed travel.

DURING THE PILOT
Passengers pay the ride fare directly to the driver in cash. One Ridely booking credit covers one accepted reservation, even for multiple seats. New rider accounts receive two complimentary booking credits. Additional credits are listed at $3.99 CAD each; native purchases are not enabled in this release. The Cart screen shows booking-credit information and balance; it does not collect the cash ride fare.

Share an expiring itinerary link with someone you trust. This shares trip details, not live GPS location. Driver checks do not guarantee driving behaviour, vehicle condition or safety.

Available in English and French. The pilot is for adults aged 18 and over. Support: support@ridely.ca. For immediate danger, call 911.
