## Deadline implication

If the newly created personal Google account is subject to 14 continuous days of qualifying closed testing, beginning 29 October 2026 reaches 14 days on 12 November, not 4 November. Tester-count, continuity, production-access application and approval rules must be verified in the new account. Organisation verification and Apple enrollment/review can also take longer than the target window. Do not promise a public 4 November release before account eligibility and approval exist.
