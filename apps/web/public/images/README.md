# Ridely marketing photography

These three original AI-generated marketing images recreate the scenes in the approved electric-lime homepage concept. They are illustrative imagery, not photographs of actual Ridely customers or verified drivers.

- `ridely-friends.webp`: friends meeting beside a car in Montréal at dusk.
- `ridely-rider.webp`: a shared road-trip passenger scene.
- `ridely-city.webp`: Montréal bridge/skyline through a passenger window.

Images are served locally as compressed WebP. Text, buttons, route doodles and promotional content are live HTML/SVG/CSS, not embedded screenshots. Profile photos uploaded by members are a separate feature and are not used for marketing.
